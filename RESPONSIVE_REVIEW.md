# Responsive Review

## Tablet Header Finding

Testing at the iPad Pro portrait viewport confirmed that the full desktop navigation activated at 1024 pixels before sufficient horizontal space was available for the brand lockup, eight navigation links, and contact call-to-action.

## Resolution

The desktop navigation breakpoint is moved from `lg` to `xl`. Tablet-width viewports retain the accessible compact menu, while wider landscape tablets and desktop monitors display the complete navigation.
